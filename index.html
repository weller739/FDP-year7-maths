Best starting point

Start with the Support group if students need confidence, then move into Consolidate and Extend.

Class focus

Converting between fractions, decimals and percentages appears in all three groups because it has been a major class focus.

How the missions work

One mission, three stages

1

Learn

Students are taught the skill with step-by-step notes and worked examples.

2

Practice

Students answer guided questions and receive correct/incorrect feedback plus hints and reteaching.

3

Quiz

Students complete a short quiz, view their score, and download a Word-compatible evidence file.

Mission pathways

Each pathway contains four missions across fractions, decimals and percentages.

Support

Build confidence with the core ideas

Ideal for students who need strong examples, simpler numbers and more guided practice.

Consolidate

Strengthen key Year 7 skills

Good for students ready to connect skills and handle mixed question types.

Extend

Apply and reason with more challenge

Suitable for students who are ready to stretch their understanding with more complex examples.
